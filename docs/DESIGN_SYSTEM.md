# Design System

Goal: a modern, professional, quiet network operations dashboard.

## Principles

- Prioritize hierarchy, legibility, and dense scanning.
- Use restrained borders, neutral surfaces, and clear states.
- Avoid decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized marketing layouts, and
  excessive cards.
- Support light and dark modes.
- Design primarily for desktop, without breaking on tablet or mobile.

## States

Each main flow should define loading, success, empty, degraded, error, and
offline states.
